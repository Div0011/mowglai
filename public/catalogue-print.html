A deep dive into how Mowglai engineered an immersive, luxury architectural portfolio platform that converted casual website visitors into high-ticket interior design contracts.

LIVE PLATFORM: MOREINTERIOR.IN

Luxury Living Spaces & Studio Showcase

More Interior is a luxury interior architecture studio executing bespoke residential and commercial spaces. They required a digital identity that resonated with high-net-worth clients.

Consultation Inquiries

The Challenge

  • High-resolution 4K spatial photography was causing conventional portfolio websites to choke and take 6+ seconds to load.
  • Visitors on mobile devices experienced erratic layout shifts and unresponsive galleries.
  • Lack of an intentional lead capture funnel resulted in lost client inquiries and poor qualification.

The Mowglai Solution

  • Engineered responsive AVIF/WebP progressive image pipelines with blur-up placeholders for instant rendering.
  • Designed a dark-luxe aesthetic matching high-end architectural digests with subtle tactile hover physics.
  • Built an integrated consultation funnel qualifying visitors by project budget, location, and aesthetic preferences.
